# Smart View, saved views, dashboard, create order
Answers: what staff see on landing and in order lists, and what can be tailored.
Tags: [KB] KB read in full, [M] catalogue.

## Smart View (landing after sign-in)
Sections of up to 10 latest orders, fixed sort: My Orders In Progress; Delegated to Me In Progress; My Team's Unassigned Orders; My Team's Orders In Progress; Following In Progress; My/My Team's/Following In Fulfillment; My/Following Recently Fulfilled (last 168 hours). They are driven by the status flags Allow Editing and Working/Not Shipped and by permissions. Section colours are fixed. Tools: Assign To Me, Assign To, Unassign Orders, Follow/Unfollow, Print (receipt). Row: Quick View, Open Order, Assign To, Duplicate Order (with or without shipping). [KB]
Customization: choose statuses' flags; no section editor exists. [I]

## Saved views and Manage Orders
Order views: All Orders, My Orders, My Team's Orders, Managed Orders, Delegated to Me, Followed Orders; filters and column choice; save a view (columns, filters, sort), share with user groups or everyone (permissions), preferred views 1 to 3. [M]

## Create order
Store and Order Type mandatory, Account optional (only when the store has several), Customer mandatory (type three letters). Buttons Save & Edit, Save & Continue, Cancel. Needs Create Orders. Origin shows Commerce Portal. [KB]

## Dashboard
Opened by address, not in the menu: latest 10 orders and metric tiles (My Active Orders, Total Open Orders, My Groups Orders, one tile still labelled "Metric Name"). [M]

## Profile
Personal information (username read-only), managers, delegates, change password, log out. Remember Me keeps credentials 48 hours. [KB]
